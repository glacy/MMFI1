Simplificando la expresi\'on 
$$1-e^{i\delta}=e^{i\delta/2}(e^{-i\delta/2}-e^{i\delta/2})=e^{-i\delta/2}\cdot 2i \sin \frac{\delta}{2} $$
y similarmente
$$1-e^{in\delta}=e^{in\delta/2}(e^{-in\delta/2}-e^{in\delta/2})=e^{in\delta/2}\cdot 2i \sin \frac{n\delta}{2}, $$
de manera que 
$$\displaystyle \frac{e^{it}(1-e^{in\delta})}{1-e^{i\delta}}=\frac{e^{it}e^{in\delta/2}}{e^{i\delta/2}} \frac{\sin(n\delta/2)}{\sin(\delta/2)}=e^{i\{t+[(n-1)/2]\delta \}} \frac{\sin(n\delta/2)}{\sin(\delta/2)},$$
por lo tanto, la parte imaginaria de la serie, es 
$$\sin \left(t+\frac{n-1}{2}\delta \right)\sin \frac{n\delta}{2}  \left/ \sin \frac{\delta}{2}\right.$$
