Demuestre que, en coordenadas polares, 
- $\displaystyle\frac{d \hat{e}_r}{dt}= \hat{e}_\theta \frac{d \theta}{dt} $
- $\displaystyle\frac{d \hat{e}_\theta}{dt}= -\hat{e}_r \frac{d \theta}{dt} $
