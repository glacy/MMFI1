## Video direction

**Palette system:** primary → azul (vector A), secondary → verde (vector B), accent → rojo (resultados), background → azul oscuro 10% (campo 3D), neutral → grises para texto.

**Motion grammar:** long-tail eases (power3), VO-paced reveal model (cada elemento aparece en su spoken cue), held beats al 30% de frames, stillness > bad motion.

**Rhythm:** Frame 1 reveals → Frame 2 held beat → Frame 3 resolves. No front-loading, no slideshow mode.

**Negative list:** No purple-blue "AI" gradients, no generic bokeh, no lazy breathing, no cursor (faceless explainer).
